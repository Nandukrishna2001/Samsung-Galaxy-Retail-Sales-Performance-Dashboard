# 📱 Samsung Galaxy Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Tableau Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project** developed to analyze Samsung Galaxy smartphone retail sales performance across different product series, models, regions, and network technologies.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Samsung Galaxy's retail sales ecosystem by examining **total units sold, revenue generation, average selling price, 5G market share, Galaxy series performance, regional sales distribution, and individual model performance**.

The project transforms structured smartphone retail sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI indicators, comparative analysis, and dynamic filtering.

---

# 🔍 Key Dashboard Analysis

### 📈 1. Sales Trend Analysis

The **Sales Trend** visualization examines Galaxy smartphone sales across different years, covering the period from 2019 to 2025.

The chart provides a chronological view of unit sales and helps identify changes in sales performance over time. It allows users to understand how Samsung Galaxy's retail sales evolved across different product generations and market periods.

Year-based filtering makes it possible to focus on particular periods and investigate changes in sales volume.

### ⚡ 2. 5G vs 4G Sales by Galaxy Series

This analysis compares the sales performance of 5G and 4G smartphones across the Galaxy A, S, and Z series.

The visualization highlights the contribution of different network technologies within each product category, providing an understanding of the transition towards 5G-enabled Galaxy devices.

The comparison helps examine how 5G adoption differs across product segments and how the Galaxy series contribute to the overall 5G sales distribution.

### 🌍 3. Regional Sales Distribution

The **Units Sold by Region** section compares Samsung Galaxy smartphone sales across major geographic markets.

The dashboard covers:

- Asia-Pacific
- Europe
- India
- Latin America
- Middle East & Africa
- North America

This analysis helps identify regional differences in sales volume and understand how different markets contribute to the overall retail performance.

### 🏆 4. Top 10 Galaxy Models

The **Top 10 Galaxy Models** visualization uses a treemap to highlight the highest-performing smartphone models based on units sold.

It provides a comparative view of individual model performance and identifies the devices contributing significantly to Samsung Galaxy's overall sales volume.

The visualization includes popular Galaxy models such as Galaxy S20, Galaxy S20 Ultra, Galaxy S10+, Galaxy A52 5G, Galaxy S21+, Galaxy S21, Galaxy S20+, Galaxy S10, Galaxy A32 5G, and Galaxy S21 Ultra.

### 🍩 5. Sales Share by Galaxy Series

This visualization examines the contribution of the Galaxy A, S, and Z series to total smartphone unit sales.

The series-wise comparison provides a clear understanding of how different product categories contribute to the overall sales portfolio.

It also allows users to compare the relative market contribution of Samsung's flagship, mid-range, and foldable smartphone categories within the analyzed dataset.

### 💰 6. Revenue by Galaxy Series

The **Revenue by Galaxy Series** chart compares revenue generation across the Galaxy A, S, and Z product categories.

This analysis helps identify which series contribute most significantly to total revenue and understand the relationship between product categories, unit sales, and revenue generation.

By comparing revenue and unit sales together, the dashboard provides a broader perspective on the commercial performance of different Galaxy product segments.

# 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze Samsung Galaxy smartphone retail sales and understand the factors influencing product-level, series-level, and regional sales performance.

Rather than examining only total revenue or units sold, the dashboard connects **product performance, Galaxy series contribution, regional distribution, 5G adoption, average selling price, and sales trends** into a single analytical environment.

The project provides a structured approach to understanding smartphone retail performance and demonstrates how data visualization can transform sales records into meaningful business insights.

---

# 💡 Analytical Value

The dashboard demonstrates how smartphone retail sales data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence solution.

By connecting **units sold, revenue generation, product categories, regional distribution, and network technology**, the project provides a broader understanding of Samsung Galaxy's retail sales performance.

The analysis also demonstrates why evaluating sales through multiple dimensions is important. A product's sales volume alone does not fully explain its commercial contribution; revenue, average price, regional performance, and technology adoption provide additional perspectives.

Overall, the project showcases the application of Tableau and data analytics techniques to a real-world consumer electronics retail use case.
